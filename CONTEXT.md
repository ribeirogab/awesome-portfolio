# Portfolio

The portfolio presents a fictional creative professional through their experience, projects, skills, and contact invitation.

## Language

**Portfolio Owner**:
The fictional professional represented by the portfolio. The initial Portfolio Owner is John Doe.
_Avoid_: User, account, profile owner

**Section**:
An ordered, self-contained block of the portfolio page. The Portfolio Owner chooses which Sections exist and in what order.
_Avoid_: Block, widget, page area

**Entry**:
A titled item listed inside a Section, such as an experience, an education, or a project.
_Avoid_: Item, card, row

**Experience**:
A time-bound professional engagement attributed to the Portfolio Owner.
_Avoid_: Job, position

**Project**:
A fictional work sample that demonstrates the Portfolio Owner's capabilities.
_Avoid_: Product, case study
