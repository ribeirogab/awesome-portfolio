# Portfolio

The portfolio presents a fictional creative professional through their experience, projects, skills, achievements, and contact invitation.

## Language

**Portfolio Owner**:
The fictional professional represented by the portfolio. The initial Portfolio Owner is John Doe.
_Avoid_: User, account, profile owner

**Experience**:
A time-bound professional engagement attributed to the Portfolio Owner.
_Avoid_: Job, position

**Project**:
A fictional work sample that demonstrates the Portfolio Owner's capabilities.
_Avoid_: Product, case study

**Achievement**:
A professional recognition or milestone associated with the Portfolio Owner.
_Avoid_: Award, badge
