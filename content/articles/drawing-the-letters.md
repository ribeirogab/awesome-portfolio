## Starting over

I have set type for nine years and drawn almost none of it. This year I went back to the basics: pencil, tracing paper, and a single lowercase alphabet at a time.

The first lesson was humility. The optical corrections that make a good typeface feel even are invisible until you try to draw a letter without them. An "o" that is mathematically the same height as an "x" looks too short. Every curve needs to overshoot.

## What changed in my interface work

Drawing letters made me slower and more careful with the type I set. I notice rhythm now, not only size. I notice when a line length fights the face. I stopped reaching for letter-spacing as a fix for a face that was simply the wrong choice.

The goal is still modest: to draw the letters, not only set them. But even a modest goal changes how you look at every page.
